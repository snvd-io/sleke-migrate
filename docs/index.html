Sleke Migrate Privacy policy

Move what matters to your new phone.

Bring your contacts, photos, videos, and files over from your old Android phone. Your selected data goes directly to the new phone on the same Wi-Fi.

Install Sleke Migrate on the phone you’re moving from. During setup, your new SLEKE phone shows the code you’ll scan.

Download for Android

Latest release for Android 9 and newer.

How the move works

  1. Start setup on the new phone

    Choose to bring data from another phone. It shows a pairing code.

  2. Scan the code with this phone

    The two phones lock onto each other. No account, no sign-in.

  3. Pick what to send

    Contacts, whole albums, or folders. Keep both phones awake and on the same Wi-Fi while it sends.

Install it on your old phone

  1. Open the downloaded file

    Tap sleke-migrate.apk in your notifications or in Downloads.

  2. Allow this one install

    Android asks before installing from a browser. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.

  3. Tap Install, then open the app

    It walks you through the rest.

Private by design

The contacts, photos, and files you choose travel directly to the receiving phone over local Wi-Fi. The connection is tied to the device that showed the pairing code. The transfer does not create a cloud copy.

Read the Sleke Migrate privacy policy for permissions, diagnostics, and deletion choices.
