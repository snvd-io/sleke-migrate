Sleke Migrate

Send your contacts, photos, and files straight to your new phone over Wi-Fi. Nothing goes to the cloud.

Download the app

Latest release · Android 9 and newer

Install it

  1. 1
    Open the file you downloaded

    Tap the downloaded sleke-migrate.apk from your notifications or Downloads.

  2. 2
    Allow this one install

    Android will ask to allow installing from your browser. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.

  3. 3
    Tap Install

    Open the app and follow the steps to send your data.

How it works

  • →
    Connect to the new phone

    On the new phone, open the setup wizard and choose transfer from another phone.

  • →
    Scan the code

    Point this phone at the pairing code to lock onto the new device.

  • →
    Review and send

    Choose your contacts and photos, then send them directly.

Private by design. Your data transfers phone-to-phone over your local Wi-Fi, with the connection pinned to the new device. It is never uploaded to a server.

Runs on the old phone you are moving from · Android 9+
