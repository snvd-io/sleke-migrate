# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-13

A redesign of both phones' screens, built on the SLEKE. design system.

- One big number to watch while moving: the size you picked on the old phone, the progress on both phones, and a checklist that ticks off contacts, photos, videos, music and files.
- Pick exactly which contacts move. Tap Contacts to choose them, or use its switch to include all or none. A phone without contacts doesn't offer them.
- Choosing photos shows album covers and a live total.
- Time left now shows on transfers with many photos.
- If a transfer stops, both phones show what's already safe and offer Send the rest. If only contacts arrived, the new phone says so.
- Larger text, white surfaces in light mode, and buttons that clearly show when they're off.
- On iPad and tablets, the screens sit in a centred column.
- Sizes like "1.6 GB" no longer break across two lines.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-12

The first release of SLEKE. Migrate as `io.sleke.migrate`, on Google Play, the App Store and GitHub. It replaces the earlier 0.0.x test builds, which used the package name `com.sleke.migrate`. If one of those is installed, install this release and remove the old one.

- Move contacts, photos, videos and files from your old phone to your new SLEKE. phone, straight over your Wi-Fi. Nothing goes through a cloud, and there's no account.
- Pair by scanning the code on the new phone. Can't scan? Type the code instead, and both phones show the same six digits to confirm before anything is sent.
- Choose exactly what moves: contacts, whole albums or single photos, and folders, with a live total.
- Sending shows the time left and hides it if the transfer stalls. If the transfer stops, both phones say why, and trying again skips what already arrived.
- A look from the SLEKE. design system that follows your phone's light or dark mode, and the SLEKE. Migrate icon with its full stop, including the themed and dark versions.
- The privacy policy is on the first screen.
- Requires Android 9 or newer, or iOS 16 or newer.
